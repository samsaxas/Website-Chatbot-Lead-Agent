Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How long does a typical website project take?
A: Most website projects take 3-6 weeks from kickoff to launch, depending
on the number of pages and whether custom functionality is required.

Q: Do you work with startups or only established brands?
A: We work with both. We have package options suited to early-stage
startups as well as larger, ongoing retainer engagements for established
brands.

Q: What is your typical process?
A: We start with a discovery call to understand your goals, then move into
a proposal and scope, followed by design, development/production, and
launch. Most engagements include 1-2 rounds of revisions.

Q: Do you offer ongoing support after launch?
A: Yes, we offer monthly retainer packages for ongoing website updates,
content, and digital marketing support after the initial project launches.

Q: Can you handle both branding and web design in one project?
A: Yes, many of our clients start with brand identity work and roll
directly into website design and development using the same brand system.
